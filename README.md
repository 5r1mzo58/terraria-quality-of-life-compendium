🔹 **Terraria — Quality of Life Compendium** is a powerful tool built to help you enjoy dozens of quality-of-life improvements in Terraria bundled into a single well-optimized mod. QoL Compendium addresses the most common quality-of-life complaints about Terraria in one balanced, comprehensive package.

🔸 Whether you're a seasoned professional or just getting started, **Terraria — Quality of Life Compendium** delivers a clean, intuitive experience packed with everything you need — right out of the box. ## 🚀 Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Autoswing | All melee weapons gain autoswing without changing their stats |
| Item Tooltips | Enhanced item tooltips with crafting paths and drop locations |
| Quick Stack | Improved quick stack to nearby chests with categorization |
| Boss Health Bars | Visual health bars for all bosses during encounters |
